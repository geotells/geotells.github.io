Delete your GeoTells account

Last updated: 13 November 2026

GeoTells is an application by Damián Delgado. Signing in with a Google account is optional; if you did, you can delete your account and the data linked to it whenever you like, in either of these two ways.

In the application

  • Open GeoTells and go to Settings → Your progress.
  • Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account and its data are deleted from our server at once. The progress on your phone stays there; it is erased when you uninstall the application or clear its data.

Without the application

Write to hello@geotells.com from the email address of the Google account you signed in with, with the subject “Delete my account”. If you write from another address, we will ask you to confirm it from that account. We delete the account within 30 days at most, usually sooner, and reply once it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your account: the email address, name, profile picture address and Google account identifier.
  • Your nickname, if you chose one.
  • The copy of your progress on our server.
  • Your pending reminders and your device’s push address.
  • The days played on record.
  • Your daily challenge and word of the day results.
  • The logs of your lessons and of the welcome tour.

What is kept

Question reports you sent and the application’s crash reports stay with no link to you, as they are used to fix the application: the first until the issue is resolved (one year at most), the second for 90 days. Aggregate figures, such as how many players took a challenge, identify no one and do not change.

If you have no account

Without an account, what our server keeps goes under an anonymous identifier. To erase it, turn off “Count my days played”, “Keep a copy online” and reminders in Settings, then uninstall the application; or write to hello@geotells.com with the transfer code shown in Settings, so we can find it and delete it.

All the details are in the privacy policy.
